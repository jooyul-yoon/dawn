# Historical source notes

The question set deliberately keeps the historical descriptions concise and
non-graphic. These sources were used to check the core clues and are a good
place to investigate each dossier further.

- [Polybius, *Histories*, Book 3: Cannae](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0234%3Abook%3D3)
- [Musée de l'Armée: Battle of Agincourt](https://www.musee-armee.fr/ExpoChevaliersBombardes/battle-agincourt.html)
- [Napoleon Foundation: The retreat from Russia](https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/timelines/napoleons-russian-campaign-the-retreat/)
- [RAF Museum: How radar worked in the Battle of Britain](https://www.rafmuseum.org.uk/research/online-exhibitions/history-of-the-battle-of-britain/how-radar-works/)
- [U.S. Naval History and Heritage Command: Midway intelligence](https://www.history.navy.mil/content/dam/nhhc/about-us/leadership/hgram_pdfs/H-Gram_006.pdf)
- [U.S. Marine Corps: objectives and hazards at Inchon](https://www.marines.mil/Portals/1/Publications/The%20Sea%20Services%20in%20the%20Korean%20War%201950-1953%20%20PCN%2019000412100_1.pdf?ver=2012-10-11-164123-027)
